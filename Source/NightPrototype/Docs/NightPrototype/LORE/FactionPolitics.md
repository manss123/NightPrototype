# Faction Politics

## Document Role

This document expands the political behavior of factions defined in [[LoreCanon]] and [[WorldBible]].

It should not introduce a faction as mandatory demo scope unless that faction also creates a playable event, memory, or consequence.

## Purpose

This document defines the political shape of the world.

The setting should not be simple humans versus monsters.

Core rule:

```text
No faction is purely good or purely evil.
Every faction has fear, need, history, enemies, and possible memory-driven consequences.
```

## Political Axes

The world is shaped by several overlapping struggles:

```text
Survival Politics
Humans trying to survive the night.

Blood Politics
Vampires controlling people through blood, debt, fear, and dependency.

Beast Politics
Werewolves controlling territory through pack law and instinct.

Death Politics
Undead and revenants making death incomplete.

Machine Politics
Old World AI and machine cults influencing the world from below.

Memory Politics
The player's actions changing relationships between people, enemies, and factions.
```

## Major Conflicts

```text
Human Survival vs Monster Rule
Vampire Blood Order vs Werewolf Wild Order
Old World Machine vs Living Flesh
Hunters' Purity vs Witches' Pragmatism
Dead Memory vs Living Guilt
Noble Control vs Feral Hunger
```

## Faction Overview

| Faction | Temporary Allies | Main Enemies | Complication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human Settlements | Hunters, Witches | Feral Vampires, Zombies | Fear Dhampir, depend on forbidden help |
| Hunters / Witchfinders | Human Settlements | Vampires, Werewolves, Undead | Distrust Witches and Dhampir |
| Witches / Blood Alchemists | Humans, Dhampir | Some Hunters | Trade with dangerous factions |
| Noble Vampires | Ghouls, Blood Thralls | Werewolves, Hunters | Treat humans as resources |
| Feral Vampires | Almost none | Almost everyone | Sometimes used by Noble Vampires |
| Dhampir | Witches, some settlements | Hunters, Vampire purists | Caught between factions |
| Ghouls / Blood Thralls | Noble Vampires | Hunters | Some still love their old human lives |
| Werewolf Packs | Old Forest Kin | Vampires, Hunters | Some packs can be negotiated with |
| Broken Wolves | Witches, some packs | Hunters, themselves | Victims as much as threats |
| Old Forest Kin | Werewolves | AI, Old World remnants | Distrust humans |
| Zombies | None | Everyone | May be controlled by AI or cults |
| Undead / Revenants | Witches, some revenant houses | Hunters, AI | Some want release, some want memory |
| Machine Church / AI Cult | AI, Machine-Risen | Hunters, Werewolves | Can infiltrate settlements |
| Old World Remnants | AI systems | Intruders | Hold the truth of the world |

## Human Settlements

Humans are weak but central.

They survive through:

```text
walls
fire
ritual
trade
fear
compromise
```

Internal conflicts:

```text
purists who want all non-humans dead
pragmatists willing to bargain
faithful who believe in strange powers
desperate people willing to sell others for safety
```

Memory-driven hooks:

```text
PlayerSavedVillage -> trust
PlayerFledDuringAttack -> distrust / redemption
PlayerFailedToSaveChild -> grief / blame
PlayerBroughtMonsterHere -> settlement closes gates
```

## Hunters / Witchfinders

Hunters protect humanity, but can become brutal.

Internal groups:

```text
Purists
Pragmatists
Inquisitors
Relic Hunters
```

Hooks:

```text
PlayerKilledVampire -> hunters become interested
PlayerSparedDhampir -> hunters distrust player
PlayerUsedForbiddenRelic -> hunters investigate
PlayerKilledHunter -> hunter order escalates
```

## Witches / Blood Alchemists

Witches are healers, alchemists, curse doctors, and keepers of dangerous knowledge.

They are useful, feared, and politically unstable.

Internal groups:

```text
Healers
Blood Alchemists
Grave Doctors
Forbidden Practitioners
```

Hooks:

```text
PlayerSavedInfectedNPC -> witch opens rare treatment
PlayerBetrayedWitchToHunters -> revenge / curse
PlayerBroughtRareBlood -> unlocks monster weakness research
```

## Noble Vampires

Noble Vampires are stable high-blood lines.

They rule through:

```text
blood tax
thralls
debt
fear
political bargains
old city control
```

Possible houses:

```text
House Sanguis
House Nocturne
House Ashveil
House Carrion
```

Hooks:

```text
PlayerKilledFeralVampire -> noble may reward or manipulate player
PlayerKilledVampireNoble -> house retaliation
PlayerAcceptedBloodDeal -> humans distrust player
PlayerExposedBloodFarm -> vampire faction escalates
```

## Feral Vampires

Feral Vampires are unstable, hungry, and useful as early threats.

They should not always be mindless.

Some may be:

```text
fallen nobles
failed experiments
lost villagers
weapons released by nobles
```

They are ideal for early nemesis-lite encounters.

## Werewolf Packs

Werewolves are territorial rather than purely evil.

Their politics are about:

```text
pack
territory
hunting law
instinct
old forest rules
```

Important distinction:

```text
Vampires infiltrate society.
Werewolves enforce territory.
```

## Undead / Revenants

Undead are a moral problem, not only enemies.

Different factions see them differently:

```text
Hunters want to burn them.
Witches want to understand them.
Machine Church wants to use them.
Revenants want to be remembered.
Humans want to forget them.
AI treats them as usable data.
```

## Machine Church / AI Cult

This is a late-game truth layer.

Machine Church is the organized religion/faction built around underground machine signals.

AI Cult / Machine Faith is the broader belief phenomenon.

Both connect to the Ancient AI and Old World Remnants, but most followers do not understand the true source.

Hooks:

```text
AI studies player behavior
machine cult infiltrates village
old facility changes enemy behavior
memory records become part of world-level manipulation
```

## Design Rule

Faction politics should generate events and consequences, not just lore.

Every faction should be answerable through:

```text
What do they fear?
What do they need?
Who do they use?
Who do they hate?
What memory would change their behavior?
```
